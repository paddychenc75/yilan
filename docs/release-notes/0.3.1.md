## 中文

### 改进

- **RAW 翻页更跟手**：翻到下一张时会顺着你翻页的方向提前准备后面两张，高清画面的解码改为排队进行，不再互相争抢。照片多、文件大或电脑较慢时，下一张出来得更快。
- **删除更好找**：顶部「⋯」菜单里加了「移到废纸篓」，和按 `⌫` 一样，会先确认，删除后可以按 `⌘Z` 撤销。
- **macOS 15 上文字更清楚**：照片很亮时，顶部的文件名、文件夹名和按钮现在更容易看清。
- **窗口左上角按钮更稳**：改进了红黄绿三个按钮在 macOS 15 上的显示，避免被窗口上沿切掉一半。
- **保存旋转更省内存**：给一亿像素这样的超大照片保存旋转时，占用的内存大幅减少。
- **打开大文件夹更快**：文件夹里有几千张照片时，读取星级不再让界面卡一下。

### 修复

- **信息面板少一颗星**：在 macOS 15 上，没有拍摄信息的照片（例如扫描件）打开信息面板时，第五颗星和个别名称不显示，现在显示完整。
- **清除筛选后跳到第一张**：筛选结果为空时点「清除筛选」，以前会跳到文件夹第一张，现在回到你刚才在看的那张。
- **编辑图标没对齐**：顶部的编辑图标比旁边的图标略低，现在对齐了。
- **设置里的空页**：「缓存与性能」这一页还没有内容，先不显示。

## English

### Improved

- **Smoother RAW browsing**: when you move to the next photo, the app now prepares the next two in the direction you are going, and full-quality decodes wait in a queue instead of competing. With many photos, large files or a slower Mac, the next photo appears sooner.
- **Delete is easier to find**: the "⋯" menu in the top bar now has "Move to Trash". It works like pressing `⌫`: it asks first, and `⌘Z` undoes it.
- **Clearer text on macOS 15**: on very bright photos, the file name, folder name and buttons at the top are now easier to read.
- **Steadier window buttons**: improved how the red, yellow and green buttons are shown on macOS 15, to keep them from being cut off by the top edge of the window.
- **Save Rotation uses less memory**: saving the rotation of very large photos, such as 100-megapixel files, now needs far less memory.
- **Large folders open faster**: in folders with thousands of photos, reading ratings no longer makes the window stutter.

### Fixed

- **Missing star in the info panel**: on macOS 15, for photos without camera data (scans, for example), the fifth star and some labels in the info panel were not drawn. They now show in full.
- **Clear Filters jumped to the first photo**: when a filter had no results, clicking "Clear Filters" used to jump to the first photo in the folder. It now returns to the photo you were viewing.
- **Edit icon was misaligned**: the edit icon in the top bar sat slightly lower than its neighbours. It is now aligned.
- **Empty settings page**: the "Cache & Performance" page has no options yet, so it is hidden for now.

## 安装 / Install

**系统要求**：macOS 15（Sequoia）或更高版本，Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片都可以。

**首次打开**：这个版本只做了 ad-hoc 签名。第一次打开被拦下后，到「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」点「仍要打开」，或在终端执行 `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Yilan.app`。

**Requirements**: macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

**First launch**: this build is ad-hoc signed only. After it is blocked once, click "Open Anyway" in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Yilan.app`.
